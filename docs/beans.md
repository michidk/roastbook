# Coffee database and bags

A bean is a permanent coffee profile. You can save coffees you tried at a café
without buying a bag, then add a bag later if you buy the same coffee.

## Your shelf

When adding a coffee, **Create and track a bag** reveals purchase details such as
weight, roast date, and price. Turn it off to save only the coffee profile.

**Always on shelf** keeps a coffee in the prominent shelf collection without
requiring an active bag. Both controls can be enabled together.

A coffee stays on the shelf while Always on shelf is enabled or any of its bags
has not been archived. An empty bag stays active until you archive it yourself.
Archiving the last bag moves the coffee into Archive unless Always on shelf is
on. The Archive also holds coffees recorded only during café visits. History
remains available there, and you can add a new bag at any time.

Search and the bean-tag filter include both shelf and archived coffees. Brew
selection offers active bags first and also allows selecting a coffee without a
bag. Those brews have no bag inventory deduction.

## Ratings and notes

Each coffee can have a personal rating of 1–5 stars and a rating note. Without a
personal rating, Roastbook displays the average of its rated brews across all
bags. Unrated brews do not affect the average. The source and rated brew count
appear beside the rating. A coffee without either source displays Not rated.

Set or clear the personal rating in the coffee editor. Selecting the same star
again clears it and restores the brew average. The rating note remains separate
from the coffee's descriptive notes and can be saved without a rating.

Café-visit ratings describe the visit and do not enter the brew average. Use the
personal bean rating to record an opinion about a coffee tried at a café.

## Bean tags

Manage bean tags in Settings under Taste profile. Each journal starts with
Reorder and Want to try. You can rename or delete these defaults and add custom
tags. Deleted tags never reappear automatically. Deleting a tag removes its
assignments and keeps the coffees and their history.

Assign tags when creating or editing a coffee. Bean tags organize your database;
tasting tags describe the flavors of a brew or café visit. Tags do not change
shelf visibility.

## Logging coffee at a café

In Log visit, search the Coffee field to reuse an existing coffee. Type a name
and choose Add as a new coffee to enter a new one inside the visit form. Only
the coffee name is required. The roaster is optional, and More coffee details
holds ratings, tags, pictures, and other profile or bag details.

New café coffees default to no bag and no shelf placement. Enable bag tracking
or Always on shelf if you also bought the coffee. Leave the coffee field empty
when you do not know which beans the café used.

Saving creates the coffee and visit together, including an optional bag and tag
assignments. Canceling creates no coffee. Pictures upload after the records
save; failed uploads offer retry or continuing without pictures. Local journals
save these changes through the same offline queue as other journal entries.
